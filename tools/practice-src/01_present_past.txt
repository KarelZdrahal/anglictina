@text
Martin has worked as a nurse in a large hospital for six years. He didn't plan to become a nurse: at university he studied economics, but he hated sitting in an office all day. One evening, while he was visiting his grandmother in hospital, he talked to a nurse who was looking after her. She told him how much she enjoyed her job. A year later, Martin started a nursing course. Today he usually works night shifts, which he finds tiring, but he says he has never regretted his decision.
@q ## What did Martin study at university? ## economics / medicine / nursing ## economics
@q ## What gave him the idea to change his career? ## a conversation with a nurse / a film about hospitals / his teacher's advice ## a conversation with a nurse
@q ## How does Martin feel about his decision now? ## He doesn't regret it. / He wants to go back to an office. / He isn't sure. ## He doesn't regret it.
@end
@text
When I was a teenager, my family used to spend every August in a small cottage in the mountains. There was no internet and the TV only had two channels, so we spent most of the day outside. My sister and I would go swimming in the lake or pick mushrooms in the forest. In the evenings, my dad would light a fire and tell us stories. The cottage was sold years ago, but I still think about those summers whenever I smell wood smoke.
@q ## What did the family NOT have at the cottage? ## internet / a lake nearby / a TV ## internet
@q ## What did the dad do in the evenings? ## told stories by the fire / watched TV / went fishing ## told stories by the fire
@q ## What reminds the writer of those summers today? ## the smell of wood smoke / photos / mushrooms ## the smell of wood smoke
@end
