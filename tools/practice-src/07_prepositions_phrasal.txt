@text
Last Saturday we set off early for a trip to the mountains. Unfortunately, our car broke down on the motorway, so we had to call for help. While we were waiting, it started to rain. Despite this, we didn't give up. A mechanic arrived after an hour and fixed the problem quickly. We finally got to the hotel in the evening, tired but happy. The next day the weather cleared up and we spent the whole day hiking. In the end, it turned out to be a great weekend.
@q ## What happened on the motorway? ## The car broke down. / They had an accident. / They got lost. ## The car broke down.
@q ## How long did they wait for the mechanic? ## about an hour / all day / ten minutes ## about an hour
@q ## What was the weather like the next day? ## It cleared up. / It rained all day. / It snowed. ## It cleared up.
@end
@text
Many people find it hard to get up early. However, scientists say that some of us are simply "night owls". Although night owls often feel tired in the morning, they are usually more creative in the evening. On the other hand, "early birds" tend to be more productive before lunch. Because of this, some companies now let employees choose their working hours. This means that night owls can start later, while early birds can finish work in the afternoon.
@q ## When are night owls usually more creative? ## in the evening / in the morning / before lunch ## in the evening
@q ## What have some companies done? ## let employees choose their hours / closed in the morning / stopped working in the evening ## let employees choose their hours
@q ## Who can finish work in the afternoon? ## early birds / night owls / managers ## early birds
@end
