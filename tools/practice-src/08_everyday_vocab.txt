@text
Lucie works in a busy restaurant. She earns a good salary, but she often does overtime and rarely has a day off. Last month she made a big decision: she asked her boss if she could work four days a week instead of five. She knew that she would earn less, but she wanted to spend more time with her family. To her surprise, her boss agreed. Now Lucie is less tired and she has time to do the things she enjoys, like gardening and baking.
@q ## What problem did Lucie have? ## She rarely had a day off. / Her salary was too low. / She didn't like her boss. ## She rarely had a day off.
@q ## What did she ask her boss for? ## a four-day week / more money / a new job ## a four-day week
@q ## How does Lucie feel now? ## less tired / bored / worried about money ## less tired
@end
