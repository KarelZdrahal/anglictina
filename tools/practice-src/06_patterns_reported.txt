@text
Last week I went for a job interview at a travel agency. The manager asked me why I wanted to work there and what experience I had. I told her that I had worked in a hotel for two years and that I enjoyed meeting people from different countries. Then she asked if I could speak any foreign languages. I said I spoke English and a bit of German. At the end, she promised to let me know by Friday. On Thursday she called and said that they had decided to offer me the job!
@q ## Where had the writer worked before? ## in a hotel / in a travel agency / in a school ## in a hotel
@q ## Which languages does the writer speak? ## English and some German / only English / English and French ## English and some German
@q ## When did the manager call? ## on Thursday / on Friday / the next week ## on Thursday
@end
@text
A recent survey asked teenagers how they spend their free time. Most of them said they enjoyed chatting with friends online, but many admitted that they spent too much time on their phones. About half said they would like to do more sport, and a third said they wanted to learn a new skill, such as cooking or playing an instrument. When asked what stops them, most answered that they didn't have enough time because of homework.
@q ## What do most teenagers enjoy? ## chatting with friends online / doing sport / cooking ## chatting with friends online
@q ## What would about half of them like to do? ## more sport / less homework / learn an instrument ## more sport
@q ## Why don't they do these things? ## They don't have enough time. / Their parents don't allow it. / It's too expensive. ## They don't have enough time.
@end
