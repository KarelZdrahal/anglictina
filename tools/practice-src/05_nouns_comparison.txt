Choosing between living in the city and in the country isn't easy. City flats are usually smaller and more expensive than country houses, but you don't need a car, because there are plenty of buses and trams. In the country, there is much less traffic and the air is cleaner, but there are fewer shops and services. For families with young children, the country is often the most attractive option, while young people usually prefer the city because there is more to do in the evenings.
