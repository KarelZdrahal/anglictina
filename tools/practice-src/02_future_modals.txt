@text
Next month our company is moving to a new office on the other side of the city. The move is going to happen over one weekend, so nobody will have to work in a half-empty building. Staff don't have to pack their own computers – a professional company will do that – but everyone must clear their desks by Friday afternoon. The new office has a canteen and a gym, so many of us might start exercising at lunchtime. The only problem is parking: there are only forty spaces, so most people will have to come by public transport.
@q ## Who will pack the computers? ## a professional company / the staff / the IT department ## a professional company
@q ## What must everyone do by Friday? ## clear their desks / pack their computers / buy a parking ticket ## clear their desks
@q ## Why will most people use public transport? ## There aren't enough parking spaces. / It's cheaper. / The office is in the centre. ## There aren't enough parking spaces.
@end
@text
Dear Jana, thanks for your message. Yes, I'd love to come to your birthday party on Saturday! I'm working until three, but I can be at your place by five. Should I bring anything? I could make my famous chocolate cake, or I might just bring a bottle of wine – let me know what you prefer. By the way, I'm going to take the train, so I won't have to worry about driving home. See you soon! Eva
@q ## When will Eva arrive at the party? ## by five / at three / in the morning ## by five
@q ## How is Eva going to travel? ## by train / by car / by bus ## by train
@q ## What is Eva NOT sure about? ## what to bring / whether she can come / where Jana lives ## what to bring
@end
