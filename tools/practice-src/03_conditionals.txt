choose ## If it rains tomorrow, we ___ (will stay/would stay) at home. ## will stay
choose ## If I ___ (had/have) more money, I would travel round the world. ## had
choose ## I'll call you as soon as I ___ (get/will get) home. ## get
choose ## If I ___ (were/am) you, I'd accept the job offer. ## were
choose ## We won't go out ___ (unless/if) the weather improves. ## unless
choose ## What would you do if you ___ (won/win) the lottery? ## won
choose ## When she ___ (finishes/will finish) university, she wants to work abroad. ## finishes
choose ## If you don't hurry, you ___ (will miss/would miss) the bus. ## will miss
choose ## I ___ (would buy/will buy) a bigger flat if they weren't so expensive. ## would buy
choose ## Take an umbrella ___ (in case/if not) it rains. ## in case
choose ## If he ___ (doesn't/won't) phone soon, I'll start to worry. ## doesn't
